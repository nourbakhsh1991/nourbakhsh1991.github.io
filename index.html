01 / 06 · Research

Stonestacker

A mobile robot that finds natural stones, works out how to hold each one, and picks it up by itself. The long-term goal is to stack stones into dry-stone walls, with no mortar, on outdoor construction sites.

It's a Clearpath Husky A200 with an xArm 850 arm, plus a LiDAR and a camera, all running on ROS 2 on an NVIDIA Jetson Orin.

The robot is simulated in NVIDIA Isaac Sim, and its grasping is trained in Isaac Lab. This is ongoing work.

02 / 06 · Research

Excavator terrain mapping

I'm part of an Ontario Tech research project on autonomous excavators with KOCETI and KIMM in Korea. A LiDAR on the boom scans the ground as the machine digs, and from those scans we build live local and global maps of the site.

This is ongoing work. In the model, the excavator levels a mound and dumps to the side while the boom LiDAR updates the map.

03 / 06 · Web

C1 HMS

A live map of a hospital and every smart object in it. The ASP.NET Core back end converts AutoCAD DWG floor plans into primitive shapes for a web map, and the devices on that map report over Modbus. We built it for a clean room: sealed doors and pass boxes open and close on the map as they do in the room, and a click on any object shows its details. The wider hospital management system stopped at an early stage.

The model is a clean-room suite running on simulated data. It starts as the flat DWG drawing, then the devices come to life. Click a door, pass box or pressure sensor to inspect it.

04 / 06 · Embedded

C1 Control Panel

A Qt QML and C++ application for the operating-room wall panel. The surgical team controls the lights by voice, hands-free, and the panel adds anesthesia and surgery timers, an intercom, music, medical gas status and air quality, with the room hardware driven over Modbus.

The wall screen shows the panel's Lights page. Pick a command: the panel hears it, the dials update, then the surgical lights and the ceiling air flow respond.

05 / 06 · Industrial

Ahar High Level System

A WPF application that communicates with the Ahar PLC. It is the high-level layer that sits on top of the controller. Its project explorer covers the power meter, AVR, FCR and PSS controllers, set points, limiters, protection and online charts.

The monitor shows the app's power meter page. Telemetry streams from the PLC rack into its fields and online chart. Send a command and it travels back to the rack.

06 / 06 · Robotics

SixR-20

I led the two-person programming team for FUM-6R, a six-axis industrial robot built at FUM-CARE, Ferdowsi University of Mashhad. We wrote its Beckhoff PLC software in TwinCAT 2, and SixR-20, a C# WPF application for trajectory generation and robot positioning. With the engineering team, I implemented motion control, kinematics and inverse kinematics to optimize the robot's movement.

FUM-6R carries up to 20 kg at up to 3 m/s, with repeatability down to ±0.015 mm. The model follows a figure-8 path through its waypoints and solves all six joint angles every frame.
